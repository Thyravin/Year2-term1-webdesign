About Kampong Cham

Kampong Cham lies northeast of Phnom Penh and is one of Cambodia's most populated provinces. The provincial capital is a lively river town with French colonial buildings. The province is also home to Wat Nokor, an 11th-century Khmer temple with a Buddhist pagoda built inside it.

  • Province capital: Kampong Cham city
  • Main river: the Mekong
  • Famous landmark: Wat Nokor
  • Main products: rubber, rice, and corn
