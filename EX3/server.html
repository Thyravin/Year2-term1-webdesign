Things to Do in Kampong Cham

We help visitors enjoy the best of Kampong Cham. You can explore temples, ride a bicycle across the countryside, and cross the famous bamboo bridge to Koh Paen island during the dry season.

  • Visit Wat Nokor and Phnom Pros - Phnom Srei
  • Cycle across Koh Paen island
  • Walk across the Kizuna Bridge at sunset
  • Taste local food at the riverside market
